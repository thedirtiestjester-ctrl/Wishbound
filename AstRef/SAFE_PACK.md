# Wishbound AstRef Safe Pack

This pack contains the external images that were visually reviewed for inclusion in the adult Wishbound build.

## v0.4 reviewed pack

- 76 total images
- 66 environment/background images
- 10 nonsexual prop/UI/object images

The selected images contain no sexual scene content and no character sprites. School-like empty environments are treated only as neutral physical spaces and may be used as adult college, training, office, studio, or institutional locations in Wishbound.

The importer intentionally fails unless it finds exactly the reviewed 76-image set. Character and sexual-CG material from the staging archives is not copied into the APK.

Runtime assets are renamed to source-neutral Wishbound IDs:

`WB-SAFE-0001` … `WB-SAFE-0076`

The generated public manifest is:

`AstRef/manifests/packaged_safe_assets.csv`
